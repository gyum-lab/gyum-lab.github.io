Welcome! 👋

Hi, I'm Gyumin. I'm an integrated M.S.–Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at UNIST, where I work with Prof. Heechun Park in the Semiconductor Design Automation (SeDA) Lab. Before that, I received my B.S. in Electrical Engineering from Kookmin University in 2024.

My research is about making 3D chips easier to design. I work on physical design for 3D integration (F2F hybrid bonding, monolithic 3D, and heterogeneous 3D ICs), backside power and clock delivery, and machine learning for EDA, using reinforcement learning and graph neural networks for physical design. Our work on RL-based rerouting received the Best Paper Award at MLCAD 2026 🏆.

Feel free to reach out if you'd like to talk about research!
