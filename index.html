Hello, Welcome :)

My name is Gyumin Kim (김규민), an integrated M.S.–Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at UNIST, advised by professor Heechun Park in the Semiconductor Design Automation (SeDA) Lab.

My research interests include physical design automation for 3D integration, backside power delivery, power-, thermal-, and routability-aware physical design, and machine learning for EDA. My work on RL-based segment-level rerouting received the Best Paper Award at MLCAD 2026.
